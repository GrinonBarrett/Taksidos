## Lichenthrope
Composed of a mysterious organism which forms a sembiotic relationchip with fungi and plant matter to produce its vaguely human-shape form. characters get the following features;

**Creature Type**: Lichenthropes are Bigenerae. They are affected by magic as though they were Eldritch and Sylvan.

**Size**: Lichenthropes stand between 4'-6'9" tall. They can weigh between 75-100 pounds. They are medium creatures.

**Ability Score Increase**: As a lichenthrope, you have the following adjustments to your attributes  
-1 [Body]  
-1 [Inte]  
-2 [Empy]  
+3 [Fort]  
Choose two attributes other than [Fort]. They are increased by 1.

**Languages**: You gain competency in the Common Tongue and the Wild Tongue.

**Run Speed**: You may run up to 5.5 yards per move.

**Climb Speed**: You may climb up to 2.5 yards per move, presuming it is reasonable terrain to climb.

**Swim speed**: You may swim up to .5 yards per move, presuming the water isn’t too rough.

**Unarmed Attacks**: Your unarmed attacks may inflict a critcal hit as though they were cutting attacks.

### Special Features

**Amalgamated Form**: Lichenthropes cannot use armor unless it states otherwise. When unarmored, a lichenthrope is treated as having Gambeson. Fire attacks have advantage against the lichenthrope while radiant attacks have disadvantage.

**Lichenthrope Sensing**: You are, in the strictest sense, Blind- as you possess no eyes. Instead, you have a sense that lets you know the distance of creatures/objects from you a number of inches equal to your [Prcp] score + level. You can also identify the light level you are in with this sense. You gain a bonus to perceptions checks based on the light level you are in. No bonus in darkness, +1 in dimlight, +2 in fair light, and +3 in bright light.

**Strange Guard**: Choose a number between 1 and 20. Once this decision is made it cannot be changed. If you receive a Toxic or Radiant attack with an attack roll result matching that number, it cannot affect you and instead recovers a hitpoint for you.

**Synergistic Nature**: You gain a bonus to Survival checks and Medicine/Zoology checks pertaining to addressing the health of a creature equal to your current hitpoints. You suffer a penalty to all saves equal to the number of wounds you possess. You suffer a penalty to initiative for each wound you possess.

**Leichenurgy**: You have some natural power which develops over time. You learn the Leech Life spell. At 3rd level you also learn the Intoxify spell. At 6th level, you learn the greenward spell. At 8th level you learn the Bramble Grasp spell. At 10th level you learn the Hyper Rot spell.
