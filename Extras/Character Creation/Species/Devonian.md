## DEVONIAN
A near extinct race of creatures who live in isolated bodies of water or try to get by on the outskirts of fishing villages. They once worshipped a deep lord, some continuing the practice, and will instantly recognize it. Devonian characters get the following features;

**Creature Type**: Devonians are Sylvan.

**Size**: Devonians are between 5'2" and 7'10" in stature. They can weigh between 140-190 pounds. They can be medium or large creatures.

**Ability Score Increase**:  
+1 [Body]  
+1 [Rflx]  
-1 [Empy]  
Choose an attribute. It is increased by 1.

**Languages**: You gain competency in the Common Tongue, and either Hydroglossa or the Wild Tongue.

**Run Speed**: You may run up to 4.5 yards per move.

**Climb Speed**: You may climb up to 3 yards per move, presuming it is reasonable terrain to climb.

**Swim speed**: You may swim up to 7 yards per move, presuming the water isn’t too rough.

**Unarmed Attacks**: You may treat your unarmed attack as a cutting attack.

### Special Features

**Size Variation**: Denovian characters that begin as medium size get +1 to [Rflx] and [Prcp]. Devonian characters that begin as large size get +1 [Body], [Fort], and [Inte] but -1 [Empy]. These changes are to be accounted for after initial attribute generation.

**Additional Gills**: Devonians have a seperate gill system in addition to lungs which can allow them to breathe underwater.

**Rough Hide**: Devonians cannot use armor unless it states otherwise. When unarmored, a devonian is treated as having Gambeson.

**Strange Adaptation**: Whenever you perform a save, if the next save you perform is against a like effect (other than an incapacitation save), you gain advantage on that save. If the advantaged save is a critical failure- you lose 1 hit point or receive the Failing Adapatation wound, your choice. If you have no remaining hitpoints, you must choose the wound.

**Waterborne**: You are treated as proficient in Athletics while submerged. If you become proficient in Athletics, you gain advantage on Athletics related checks and saves while underwater.  
Once per encounter, when performing a [Body] or [Rflx] save while underwater- you gain advantage on that save.

**Vicious Bite**: As an additional option when you succeed a critical success while brawling, you may bite the foe whom you are brawling. This is treated as a biting attack (ignoring the penalty for the attack while brawling).
