### Z'Koulikhar
They appear to be massive earthworm with folding wings. Their "face" features a pair of tusks and the tail features a few talons. These demons are the remains of an angered dragon's consciousness. They cause great destruction and have a particular enmity towards giants and heroes of renown. The plural is Z'Koulikhain. Their collective noun is a Flight.

**Creature Type**: Z'Koulikhain are Fiends.

**Size**: Thwy have a length between 44-48 feet and a wingspan between 33-48% of their length. They weigh between 270 and 300 pounds. They are great creatures.

**Hitpoints**: A Z'Koulikhar has 7 hitpoints.

**Attributes**:  
[Body] 21 (+7)  
[Fort] 12 (+4)  
[Rflx] 19 (+6)  
[Will] 12 (+4)  
[Inte] 9 (+3)  
[Prcp] 12 (+4)  
[Empy] 6 (+2)  

**Languages**: Z'Koulikhain don't speak, but can convey the most basic of information to each other by shrieking. They also understand a number of languages equal to their [Inte] bonus.

-----

**Run Speed**: It may run up to 4.5 yards per move.

**Flying Speed**: If it is not prone, it may fly up to 37 yards per move. If must fly at least 18 yards per round to remain airborne.

**Burrow Speed**: It can burrow up to 20 yards in the earth per move.

**Unarmed Attacks**;

 • Bite: The z'koulikhar may bite a foe within 2.5 yards. This is a melee biting attack.

 • Claw: The z'koulikhar may use its talon laden tail to strike a foe within 3 yards in its rear or flank directions. This is a melee cutting attack.

**Fiendish Nature**: The z'koulikhar's fiendish nature bestows it with a few particular features;
• Do not need to eat, sleep, or breathe. They do still need to relax to rest.
• Are not subject to bleeding.
• Are immune to diseases, poisons and venom.
• Gains a bonus to all saves equal to current hitpoints.
• Are not affected by spells which would affect the flesh.

**Impaling Tusks**: When the z'koulikhar's movement would cause it to contact with another creature, it may use its reaction to inflict a piercing unarmed attack against it.

**Venomous Bite**: When the z'koulikhar successfully completes a biting attack against a foe, it also inflicts a toxic attack. If the toxic attack is successful, the creature will be subject to an acid attack after one minute and another acid attack a minute after that. This occurs for each initial toxic attack which is successful.

**Breath Weapon**:  It is capable of exhaling a toxic spay, as an action. This is a line of 154 yards in length and is a foot high and wide. Anything caught within the line suffers an acid attack. It also produces a cone whose area also overlaps with this line. The cone is 12 yards in length. Anything caught within suffers a toxic attack. Once it has used this ability, it cannot do so again for an hour.

**Dive**: If the z'koulikhar flies at least 50 yards in the previous round, it gets a bonus on its first attack made before the secondary movement phase of the current round. This bonus is equal to one-fifth of the total yards moved since the beginning of the previous round.

**Tail Talons**: When the z'koulikhar inflicts a critical hit with its Claw attack against a creature of a smaller size, they may choose to restrain that creature by grappling it.

**Infernal Regeneration**: At the end of any round the hellhare receives a wound and has any hitpoints remaining, it may use its reaction to roll a d10 and compare it to its current hitpoints. If the result is lower, it recovers a single hitpoint. It must perform a rest before doing so again.
