### Wrathful Fiend
Demons created by a powerful magic. Each of them bears a portion of some victim whose suffering is demanded to birth the vile thing. Their body looks to be shaped of clay and a caricature of the poor sod of a subject from which their birthright was drawn.

**Creature Type**: Wrathful Fiends are fiends.

**Size**: Wrathful Fiends stand between 14-15 feet and weigh 400 pounds. They are large creatures.

**Hitpoints**: A wrathful fiend has 3 hitpoints.

**Attributes**:  
[Body] 20 (+6)  
[Fort] 16 (+5)  
[Rflx] 11 (+3)  
[Will] 22 (+7)  
[Inte]  7 (+2)  
[Prcp]  8 (+2)  
[Empy]  7 (+2)  

**Languages**: The wrathful fiend does not speak.

**Run Speed**: It may run up to 10.5 yards per move.

**Climb Speed**: It may climb up to 7 yards per move, presuming it is reasonable terrain to do so.

**Swim speed**: It may swim up to 10 yard per move, presuming the water isn’t too rough.

**Flying Speed**: It may fly up to 5 yards per move.

**Unarmed Attacks**;

 • Smash: It may use any part of its body to strike a foe within 2 yards. This is a melee club attack.

 • Claw: It may use on of its two claws to slash at a foe within 2 yards. This is a melee cutting attack.

 • Bite: It can bite a foe within 1 yard, inflicting a melee bite attack.

**Woundless**: The wrathful fiend receives wounds, but not the effects of them.

**Subject of Suffering**: The Wrathful fiend is connected to a creature as a result of the magic which creates tit. It always knows the direction of that creature. Its attacks gain a +2 bonus against that creature for the purpose of determining if it is a critical hit. The wound which was suffered to create the Wrathful Fiend cannot be healed by any means, including magic, so long as the wrathful fiend still exists. WHen the wrathful fiend is destroyed, the wound can then be healed (but is not automatically restored).

**Driven by Suffering**: As reaction at the end of any round, the Wrathful Fiend may exchange its turn order chit with its subject.

**Unnatural Defense**: The wrathful fiend is not subject to critical hits from the attacks of a creature other than its subject.

**Infernal Regeneration**: At the end of any round the Wrathful Fiend receives a wound and has any hitpoints remaining, it may use its reaction to roll a d8 and compare it to its current hitpoints. If the result is lower, it recovers a single hitpoint. It must perform a rest before doing so again.

**Inverted Wound**: Whatever wound was suffered by the subject to spawn this fiend, its effect is inverted and applied to the fiend. For example, if the wound would have caused a reduction in movement speed- it does the opposite to the wrathful fiend's movement speed. If the wound would cause a penalty to something, the wrathful fiend gains a bonus. If the wound would cause the subject to lose function of features, the wrathful fiend gains use of those features.
